The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it. These include the eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. There are also dwarf planets such as Pluto, numerous moons, and millions of smaller bodies such as asteroids and comets. The Sun is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma at the center of the Solar System. It has a diameter of about 1.39 million kilometers. Gravity keeps all the objects in orbit around the Sun. Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. Mars is known as the Red Planet because of the iron oxide on its surface. Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. Saturn is famous for its prominent ring system.
